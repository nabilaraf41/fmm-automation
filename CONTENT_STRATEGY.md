# FMM Content Strategy (Oct 2026)

Targets: **USA + UK/EU** (high pay per view) and **India + Bangladesh** (high reach).
All times below are **Bangladesh time (BDT, UTC+6)**. US clocks move back on Nov 1, 2026; the times below are for after that change.

---

## 1. What works in FC Mobile content (by format)

Based on the top FC Mobile channels' most-viewed uploads (pack openings, event drops, "I packed ALL 124 OVR"
style videos) and the broader football-gaming creators (Bateson87, La5ty, Ovvy FC).

| Rank | Content type | Why it works | FMM template |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **New event / new card drop** (first 24h) | Search spike + "is he worth it?" debate | `spotlight`, `review` |
| 2 | **Redeem codes / free rewards** | Most-shared post type, people tag friends | `code` |
| 3 | **Top 10 per position** | Saves + arguments in comments | `top10` |
| 4 | **Card battles ("X or Y?")** | Fast comments, works at every follower size | `versus` |
| 5 | **Pack opening Reels** (big pull reveal) | Highest watch time on all 3 platforms | screen-record → `reel` |
| 6 | **F2P tips / meta formations** | Evergreen, gets saves on Instagram | `tip` |
| 7 | **Player review with rating** | Builds trust, people come back | `review` |
| 8 | **Community poll** | Cheap, fills gaps between bigger posts | `poll` |

**Hooks that win the first 2 seconds (Reels/Shorts):**
- Big number first: "124 OVR in a FREE pack?!"
- A question about something the viewer owns: "Is your Ronaldo worth upgrading?"
- The result shown first, then the story ("I packed him… here's how").
- Text on screen in the first frame. Most people watch muted.

**Reels length:** 7–15s for card reveals/codes (looping keeps watch time high), 25–45s for tips/Top 10.

## 2. Weekly mix (2–4 posts/day)

| Day | Photo (FB + IG) | Reel (FB + IG + YT Shorts) |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Top 10 (position of the week) | Top 10 countdown Reel |
| Tue | Card battle (versus) | Best F2P tip |
| Wed | Player review | Review Reel |
| Thu | Event/new card spotlight | Pack opening / card reveal |
| Fri | Redeem code (when available) or poll | Formation / meta tactic |
| Sat | Card battle (3 cards) | Pack opening / funny moment |
| Sun | Top 10 or "team of the week" | Weekly recap: "Best cards this week" |

On code days and new-event days, post the code/event **immediately** and push the planned post to later.

## 3. Posting times (BDT)

| Slot (BDT) | Who is awake | Post this |
|---|---|---|
| **07:00–08:00** | 🇺🇸 USA evening (8–9 pm ET / 5–6 pm PT) | **Main Reel** (highest RPM) |
| **13:00** | 🇬🇧🇪🇺 EU lunch, 🇮🇳 India afternoon | Photo (Top 10 / tip) |
| **21:00–22:00** | 🇧🇩🇮🇳 BD + India peak evening | Reel or card battle (reach) |
| **00:30** | 🇬🇧🇪🇺 UK/EU evening (6:30 pm GMT, 7:30 pm CET) | Photo or Reel (high RPM) |

- **Weekends:** gamers are on longer, so move the USA slot to **23:00 BDT** (noon ET Saturday/Sunday) and add a second US post at 07:00.
- **YouTube Shorts:** post in the **07:00 BDT** slot. Shorts keep getting views for days, so the timing matters less than on Facebook.
- After 3–4 weeks, check *Insights → Audience → Top countries / Most active times* and move the slots to match. Your own data beats any guide.

**Pay vs reach:** Facebook pays based on **where viewers are**. Indian and Bangladeshi views give reach but low pay, and US/UK/EU views pay several times more. Keep captions **in English**, and put the best Reels in the US and EU slots.

## 4. Captions

**Facebook (photo/Reel):**
```
🔥 [Hook — 1 line, the big claim or question]

[1–3 short lines of value: stats, why it matters, or the code]

👇 [Question that needs a real answer — "Who's your #1 CDM and why?"]

#FCMobile #FCMobile26 #EASportsFCMobile #FMM
```

**Instagram:** same hook, then **keywords in the caption** (Instagram search reads them: "FC Mobile 26 best CDM ranking"),
end with "Save this for your next upgrade 📌". Use **3–5 hashtags** max.

**YouTube Shorts title:** `[Big claim] | FC Mobile 26` → e.g. `Top 10 CDMs RIGHT NOW 🔥 | FC Mobile 26`
Description: 1 line + `#FCMobile #FCMobile26 #Shorts`.

**Avoid (Meta reduces reach for these):** "Like if…, Love if…" voting, "Tag 10 friends", "Share or bad luck".
Ask a real question instead; the reaction emojis on battle posts are fine as **decoration**, not as a "react to vote" command.

## 6. Growth rules
1. **Speed beats polish** on new events and codes. Being first in the first hour is the biggest growth lever.
2. **Reply to comments in the first hour.** It boosts the post on all three platforms.
3. **Make the Reel and the photo from the same post** (the `reel` script does this), so every idea ships twice.
4. **Pin** your best Reel on each profile and refresh it every week.
5. **Groups:** share 1 post per day by hand. Pick the best one, not every post.
6. **AI label on** for Flow-made images. No fake "leaks" presented as real.
