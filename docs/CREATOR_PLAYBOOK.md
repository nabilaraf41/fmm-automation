## 2. FMM vs them, ranked by views it costs us

1. **No real gameplay in most Reels.** Our Reels are animated stat cards (≈1–4k views). The top 30 are almost all
   gameplay moments, pack reveals or a face. Cost: the biggest gap, roughly 100x.
2. **Titles describe, theirs react.** "Budget XI part 3/3: the midfield (every card under 14k Star Shards) 💰" (31–2,400
   views) vs "Prime Ronaldo Bicycle Kick💀🥶". Ours are long, have no emotion and bury the player name.
3. **Too many weak posts.** 6–8 posts a day, plus duplicates (the code Reel posted twice). The algorithm rates the channel on
   its average, and top channels post 1–3 strong ones.
4. **Timing.** Our Reels go out 01:00 / 07:00 / 11:00 / 13:00 / 15:00 UTC. Vishy posts 09:40–14:00 UTC
   (15:40–20:00 BDT), Ultimate FC 16:00–20:00 UTC (22:00–02:00 BDT). Our own best hour is 21:00 BDT (score 2,629 vs 36 at 22:00).
5. **Clips that earn nothing.** "⚽ What A Goal!!" got 20–34 views: a generic title with no player name or moment.
6. **Facebook barely counts.** 143 FB Reel views in a week vs 16,910 on YouTube (weekly_report W45).
7. **Long videos get no views** (66 in 30 days), so watch hours for monetization aren't building.

What already works for us (keep): pack openings (score 1,906, best type), review Reels (1,719), Top 10 (1,124);
"I Packed R9!!" 3.6k and "I Packed Madrid 2, Got Bellingham!!" 4.2k are our best Shorts.

## 3. Rules

**Hooks (first 1–2 s)**
- Open on the payoff: the walkout, the goal, the card flip, ATB's reaction. Never a title card or a fade.
- Text on frame 0: player + number or emotion ("124 R9 😭", "EA… why? 🤡"). Most people watch muted.
- ATB's face or voice reacting in the first second.

**Titles (YouTube + Reel caption first line)**
- 2–6 words: **player + moment + 1–2 emoji**. "Prime Ronaldo Free Kick💀🥶", "2x 124 in ONE Pack 😱", "EA Scammed Me Again 😭".
- Numbers win: OVR, drafts, gems, "#3" in a series. End with "| FC Mobile" only if there's room.
- No "part 3/3", no shard prices in the title (put them in the caption).

**Covers / thumbnails**
- The cover is the peak moment (final card, the goal, ATB's shocked face) with ≤3 big words. Never black (_cover.jpg rule).
- Same layout every time so people recognise FMM: ATB top-left, card or player right, one yellow word.

**Length**
- Shorts/Reels: 10–20 s for moments and reveals, 20–30 s for a story or a pack with several cards.
  Loop the end into the start. Our 11 s stat Reels are fine in length; the content is the problem.
- Long: 10–18 min, one goal per video, as a series ("Road to 130 OVR #1", "F2P account day 7").

**Posting times (BDT)**
- YouTube Shorts: 17:00–20:00 (India/BD evening + EU afternoon) and 21:00. 07:00 stays for the US.
- Instagram Reels: 19:00–22:00.
- Facebook: 20:00–23:00 (BD/India peak).
- Long video: 18:00 (indexed before the 20:00–23:00 peak).
- Max 3–4 Reels a day per platform. A slot with nothing strong gets skipped, not filled.

**Do more of**
1. Gameplay moments from the owner's clips: legend name + moment ("Kane Free Kick💀🥶").
2. Pack openings (Thu/Sun, plus every big event in the first 24 h).
3. EA Sports Logic / bugs / "EA scam" Reels.
4. Event-day reaction Reels within 24 h (new program, Ballon d'Or, TOTW).
5. Then-vs-now / old-vs-new cards (evergreen).
6. Leaks/predictions (2 sources) and Top 10 lists.

**Do less of**
- Budget XI parts, plain "Choose 1", static stat comparisons without a twist, repeat code Reels.
  Codes: one Reel per code, showing the reward being claimed, never twice.

**Captions**
- Line 1 = the title hook. Then 1–2 lines of facts (stats, price). A real question is fine; no "like / tag / react to vote".
- 3 hashtags: #FCMobile #FIFAMobile #EAFC (+ #shorts on YouTube).
- Keyword-DM posts (studied 9 Nov, seb.ai 130 comments): line 1 = "Comment 🙌 and I'll send you <the thing> 👇"
  (one easy word/emoji, the reward named), then 2 lines with one concrete number/benefit ("checked every hour"),
  then "Save this before <moment>." 🙌 is the FMM keyword (owner: people use it, easier than a word).
  Evergreen pinned post: output/evergreen/codes_comment.* ; ManyChat sends https://nabilaraf41.github.io/fmm-codes/

## 4. Facebook growth plan
1. Check the Page first: Professional dashboard → Page status (owner, screenshot). 0–2 plays per Reel looks like
   restricted reach, not bad content: IG gets 1.4k–4.5k for the same Reel.
2. Facebook rewards original vertical video and demotes reposts/watermarks and link posts (2026 guides, not Meta itself).
   Post Reels natively, no YouTube/IG watermark, no links in the post.
3. FB groups by hand (groups stay manual). Owner data 9 Nov: the Top 10 ST photo in "FC Mobile Community" got
   99 likes + 37 comments (the Page itself gets 0-2) and group posts already earn (cents, Last 28 days earnings list).
   Daily: 2-3 posts in 3-5 different groups, a different group set each day; best = rankings/Top 10, Choose 1,
   versus, leaks, Reels. Caption ends with a debatable question ("Who's missing?"), never "like/react/tag".
4. Fewer, stronger FB posts: 2 Reels + 1 photo a day. Keep photo + Reel (owner's decision), but not 6 a day.
5. Reply to comments in the first hour.

## 6. Before every post (checklist)
- [ ] Card/event confirmed by a 2nd source (`slots.confirmed_only` / featured.json).
- [ ] ATB is in it (badge, top clip or facecam), and the clip isn't a recent repeat.
- [ ] First frame is the payoff + text; no fade from black; `_cover.jpg` exists and isn't black.
- [ ] Title: player + moment + emoji, ≤6 words, a number if there is one.
- [ ] Caption: hook, facts, 3 hashtags, no engagement bait.
- [ ] Photo + Reel on FB + IG, Reel on YT Shorts. Not already posted (posted.json).
- [ ] Posted by the cloud, never from the PC.
